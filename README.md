# THE BIG SHAKE

He had just launched himself out of the little garden pool, soaked to the skin, and picked the most dramatic possible second to shake — right in front of the camera and right in front of his little sister. She threw both hands up laughing as the spray hit her, and he just grinned through it, ears flying, like this was the best game ever invented. For one frozen instant the droplets hang around his face in a sparkling crown, and nobody in the frame is pretending to be calm. You can almost hear it — the rattle of water flying off a tiny body, a kid shrieking with laughter, and a puppy who would happily jump straight back in and do it all again.

## Why it excites

- **Jolt:** A wet puppy explodes toward the lens mid-shake, water flying straight at the viewer — you feel the splash before you even register the scene.
- **Surprise:** The droplets freeze into a glittering crown around his joyful face, turning an ordinary shake-off into a tiny coronation.
- **Momentum:** Ears out, body twisting, spray still expanding — everything in the frame is mid-motion, caught at the peak second before it all lands.
- **Thumbnail punch:** That huge, happy, soaking-wet face fills the frame with bright garden light behind it, and the laughing girl with her hands up in the back is the counted detail that makes you stop and look twice.
